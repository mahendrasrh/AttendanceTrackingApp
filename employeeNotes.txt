Many Snapdragon platforms, such as the Snapdragon 835, support the use of secure cameras with IR sensors for highly effective iris
and facial recognition, even in low-light conditions.
This technology uses infrared light patterns to map 3D depth and verify "liveness" to prevent spoofing with photos or masks.

Yes, the Qualcomm Snapdragon 835 mobile platform fully supports NFC (Near Field Communication) as a built-in feature,
making it available for contactless payments, quick data sharing, 
and other short-range wireless interactions in devices powered by this chip, as confirmed by Qualcomm's official specifications. 

Yes, the Qualcomm Snapdragon 835 absolutely supports fingerprint sensors, integrating with Qualcomm's 
Haven security platform for robust biometric authentication, including fingerprint, iris, 
and face recognition, making it a key feature in many flagship phones from that era like the Pixel 2 and OnePlus 5.
